☾ Order Process

  1. Inquiry: Send inquiry via Moon with commission type, port, IC/OC, style, and deadline.
  2. Discussion: Artist reviews scope, complexity, timeline, and total price.
  3. Confirmation & Payment: Slot is reserved upon payment receipt.
  4. Creation: Work begins with progress updates as needed.
  5. Revisions: Minor revisions included; major changes may incur extra fees.
  6. Completion: Settle remaining balance to receive final files.
Feedback and testimonials are appreciated, but optional.

☾ Terms & Conditions

Payment Terms

  • 50% down payment for orders over ₱100 (50% upon completion).
  • Full payment upfront for orders ₱100 or below.
  • Accepted via GCash, MariBank, and BPI only.
  • No refunds once work starts. 30% refund if cancelled before work begins. 100% refund if artist misses the agreed deadline.

Revisions & Agreement

  • Minor revisions: Fonts, colors, wording, spacing.
  • Major revisions: Substantial layout/composition changes (quoted separately).
  • Files are deleted 30 days after delivery—please back them up.
  • Non-commercial use only unless commercial rights are purchased (starts at ₱1,500).
