☾ About Moonlight Grid

Managed by Fourth and Noir, Moonlight Grid leverages years of editorial and formatting experience from university publications and roleplaying communities. Officially launched on December 26, 2025 (with commissions open since 2024), the shop turns client visions into affordable, custom work.

Estimated Completion Time

  • Graphics: 3 days to 1 week
  • Formats: 1 to 3 days

Note: Completion times vary depending on complexity and artist availability.

☾ Order Process

  1. Inquiry: Send an inquiry via Moon with commission type, port, IC/OC, style preference, and deadline.
  2. Discussion: Review scope, complexity, completion time, and final quote.
  3. Confirmation & Payment: Slot is reserved upon payment receipt.
  4. Creation & Revisions: Minor revisions included; major layout changes incur extra fees.
  5. Completion: Settle remaining balance to receive final files.

☾ Payment & Terms

  • Down Payment: 50% required for commissions over ₱100; 100% upfront for ₱100 or below.
  • Payment Methods: GCash, MariBank, and BPI only.
  • Refunds: No refunds once work begins. 30% refund if cancelled before work starts. 100% refund if the artist misses the agreed deadline.
  • File Retention: Delivery folders are deleted after 30 days.
  • Commercial Use: Non-commercial by default. Commercial rights start at +₱1,500.
